MonSéjour support

MonSéjour is an iPhone app that helps people from abroad manage their papers and life in France. Need help? Write to us.

Email us

app.monsejour@icloud.com

When you write

Tell us your iPhone model, your iOS version and what you were doing when the problem happened. Please don’t send documents, card numbers or passport numbers.

Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. There is nothing to sign up for. What you enter stays on your iPhone, unless you choose to back it up, share it or export it.

How do I get my information on a new iPhone?

On your old iPhone, open Settings in MonSéjour and turn on Back up to iCloud. On the new iPhone, use the same Apple Account, open Settings in MonSéjour and tap Restore from iCloud.

How much does it cost?

MonSéjour is free. There is nothing to buy in the app.

I don’t get reminders

Open the iPhone’s Settings app, then Notifications, then MonSéjour, and allow notifications. Then check your reminder times in Settings in MonSéjour.

How do I stop sharing with a helper or my family?

Stop sharing in MonSéjour, on the Trusted helper or Family screen. Do this before you delete the app.

How do I delete my information?

Turn off Back up to iCloud and stop any sharing in MonSéjour first. Then delete the app: this deletes the data stored in it.

A rule or a date looks wrong

Check the official source shown on the page, then write to us with the name of the page. Rules can change, and préfectures may ask for different papers.

Good to know

MonSéjour is an independent app. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the French government or any préfecture. It offers general information, not legal advice.
