In short

  • You don’t need an account, and we don’t ask for your email address or phone number.
  • We have no advertising and no tracking, and the app contains no analytics tools.
  • What you enter stays on your iPhone, unless you choose to back it up, share it or export it.
  • We receive something from you only if you email us, test the app through TestFlight, or share analytics with app developers in your iPhone’s settings.
  • We never sell your data.

1What stays on your iPhone

Depending on what you use, the app can store:

  • your first name, date of birth, nationality, postcode and family situation
  • your residence status, cards, visas, passport dates and applications
  • your renewals, checklists, appointments and trips
  • your answers about work, studies, French level, health cover, taxes and housing
  • names and details of family members you add
  • documents stored in the Vault, if you use it

Photos of letters and documents are read on your iPhone. We cannot see any of this information.

Please add another person’s details only if they agree, or if you are responsible for them.

If iCloud Backup is on in your iPhone’s settings, Apple may include app data in that backup. Vault document pages are left out.

2Optional iCloud features

These are off until you turn them on. They use Apple’s iCloud, and we cannot open your private iCloud data.

Back up to iCloud
your renewals, family and answers are saved in your own private iCloud. Vault documents are not included. Turning it off deletes this backup.
Trusted helper
the person you invite sees your first name, open renewals (yours and your family’s), their papers, where each application is and, unless you turn it off, the pages you added to your home. They can mark papers as ready if you allow it. They don’t see your Vault or your answers in Your situation.
Shared family
the people you invite see your first name, and see and can change the family information you share. Your own renewals stay private unless you turn on Show my renewals. Then the other adults see your open renewals, their dates, their papers and where each application is, and can’t change them. They don’t see your Vault or your answers in Your situation. If sharing stops, each person keeps a copy of the family information on their iPhone.

You choose who to invite and can stop sharing at any time. Apple shows the people in a share the name or contact details needed for the invitation.

3Emails you send us

If you email us at app.monsejour@icloud.com, we use your message only to answer you. We receive your email address and what you write, and use them based on our legitimate interest in responding. We delete emails within 24 months, unless the law requires us to keep them longer.

4Cost and downloads

MonSéjour is free. There is nothing to buy in the app, so it never asks for payment details. Apple gives us download reports that don’t identify you by name.

5Permissions

The app asks only when you use a feature:

Camera
to scan a letter or document.
Photos you choose
to read a letter or add a document. The app sees only the photos you pick.
Notifications
to remind you of deadlines. Reminders are made on your iPhone.
Calendar
to add a date you choose to your calendar.
Face ID, Touch ID or Optic ID
to unlock the Vault, if you use it. Apple never gives the app your face or fingerprint data.

You can change permissions in your iPhone’s Settings.

6Translation and links

The Translate button uses Apple’s translation feature and the guide’s general text. Apple’s privacy policy applies to it.

Links to official websites, and other websites, open outside MonSéjour. Their own privacy policies apply.

7Who helps process data

Apple provides iCloud, CloudKit, the App Store and TestFlight. Apple may process data outside the European Economic Area and is responsible for its transfer safeguards. Read Apple’s privacy policy at https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

We share data with no one else, unless the law requires it. Any other company that handles personal data for us must protect it at least as well as this policy and Apple’s rules require.

If you test the app through TestFlight, Apple sends us crash reports, basic device information and any feedback or screenshots you submit. If you agreed to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone’s settings, Apple may also send us crash reports and usage statistics. We use these only to test and fix the app.

8Keeping and deleting your data

On your iPhone
you can change or remove what you entered. Deleting the app deletes the data stored in it.
iCloud backup
turn off Back up to iCloud in the app before deleting the app.
Sharing
stop sharing in the app before deleting the app.
Emails
write to app.monsejour@icloud.com to ask us to delete them.

9Your rights

Under European data protection law, you can ask to see, correct, delete, limit or receive a copy of personal data we hold, or object to its use. If we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time. We don’t use your data to make automated decisions about you.

Write to app.monsejour@icloud.com. We reply within one month. We can’t retrieve data that exists only on your iPhone or in your private iCloud.

You can also complain to the CNIL, the French data protection authority, at https://www.cnil.fr, or to the authority where you live.

10Children

MonSéjour is meant for adults. Parents or guardians can add details about their children, which are handled as described above. We don’t knowingly collect data from children under 15. Contact us if you think a child has sent us personal data.

11Security

We rely on Apple’s security features, including iPhone data protection and encrypted connections. iCloud backup and sharing use encrypted fields for their main content. No system is completely secure.

12Changes

If we change this policy, we update this page and its date. If a change is important, we also tell you in the app.
